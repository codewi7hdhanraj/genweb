Umesh Bhavsar's 4 Pillars of Protection

The foundational principles that guide every single CCTV installation performed by GS Security Solutions.

1. Precision Architecture

No two properties are identical. Every building undergoes a structural angle survey to eliminate blind spots completely before drilling a single hole.

2. Grade-A Hardware Only

Refusing cheap counterfeit optical sensors. We only deploy certified IP67 weatherproof, Sony Starlight low-light, and H.265+ encoding chipsets.

3. Under 24h Service SLA

A broken security camera is a serious liability. Our service response team resolves critical hardware tickets within 24 hours guaranteed.

4. Strict Video Privacy

Your surveillance footage belongs strictly to you. We isolate DVR networks with encrypted routing, preventing unauthorized external access.
