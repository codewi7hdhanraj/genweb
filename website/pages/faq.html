Answering Your Questions

Everything you need to know about our CCTV equipment, installation processes, mobile application features, and warranty terms.

How long is the video recording backup stored on the NVR/DVR?

By default, we configure surveillance storage to maintain continuous 24/7 recording for 15 to 30 days depending on the HDD capacity selected (1TB to 8TB+). When storage reaches capacity, the system automatically overwrites the oldest cycle while preserving flagged event clips, or optionally mirrors footage to our secure cloud archive.

Can I access my security cameras on multiple phones simultaneously?

Yes. You can link multiple smartphones and tablets under your master GS App account. As the primary administrator, you can grant granular permissions to family members, society guards, or shift managers—allowing live viewing without giving access to delete footage or alter camera angles.

Do wireless CCTV cameras require any power cabling?

While wireless cameras transmit their high-definition video data without network cables via Wi-Fi, they still need power. We provide standard plug-in wireless models as well as solar-powered and ultra-low-power battery wireless models that require zero external wires of any kind.

What happens if the internet connection is lost?

If your local internet connection drops, the cameras continue recording uninterrupted to the local DVR/NVR hard drives without losing a single frame. Remote mobile monitoring and push notifications will pause temporarily until internet connectivity resumes, upon which historical logs will synchronize automatically.

Do you provide customized quotes and free site surveys?

Absolutely. We conduct free, no-obligation on-site security inspections for residential bungalows, housing societies, corporate offices, and warehouses. Our certified technicians will map out a custom blind-spot blueprint with an itemized budget quote matching your exact requirements.

How clear is the video during pitch-black nights?

We deploy cameras equipped with Sony Starlight optical sensors and Smart Infrared (IR) LEDs capable of illuminating up to 30 to 50 meters in zero-lux complete darkness. We also offer ColorVu full-color night vision cameras that record vivid color video even in faint moonlight.

What is included in an Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC)?

Our AMC plans include quarterly lens cleaning, cable junction weather sealing, DVR/NVR health diagnostics, firmware security updates, and priority on-call technician visits within 24 hours in case of camera dropouts.

Is the GS Mobile App free or is there a monthly subscription fee?

The core GS Mobile App is 100% free for all GS Security Solutions clients! You get lifetime unlimited live streaming, two-way audio talk, and instant intrusion alerts without any mandatory monthly subscriptions.
