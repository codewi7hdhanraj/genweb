Answering Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

Need information on products, maintenance, or app usage? Read our FAQs.

How long is the video recording backup stored on the NVR/DVR?

By default, we configure storage to hold continuous recording for 15 to 30 days depending on the HDD size you select. Once the storage is full, the system automatically overwrites the oldest footage, unless configured to back up to the cloud.

Can I access my security cameras on multiple phones simultaneously?

Yes, you can register multiple devices under the GS App system. The primary owner account can grant specific viewing and access permissions to family members or security staff.

Do wireless CCTV cameras require power cabling?

Wireless cameras transfer video feeds wirelessly over Wi-Fi, but they still require a power source. We offer battery-powered and solar-powered wireless CCTV options that require zero external cabling, alongside standard plug-in wireless models.

What happens if the internet connection is lost?

If your internet goes offline, the cameras will continue recording and saving footage locally to your physical DVR or NVR storage. However, remote mobile monitoring and instant alerts via the GS App will temporarily become unavailable until internet connection is restored.

Do you provide customized quotes for commercial structures?

Absolutely. We conduct free on-site security assessments for offices, warehouses, residential complexes, and retail centers, providing a custom layout map and itemized budget estimation matching your requirements.
